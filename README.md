# Diabetes Pharmacology Tutor

A free, self-study question bank on **pharmacologic treatment of hyperglycemia** for diabetes and endocrinology
trainees, built around the American Diabetes Association *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026* (Section 9,
Pharmacologic Approaches to Glycemic Treatment).

**Open it:** use the GitHub Pages link for this repository (Settings → Pages).

## What it contains
- 90 single-best-answer questions in a vignette style, each with two hints, a 50/50 option, a full explanation, a
  note on every option, pearls, an educational objective and references
- 6 unfolding cases that follow one patient step by step through drug choice, insulin start and intensification
- 102 flashcards of must-know doses, thresholds and facts, and 23 searchable quick-reference tables (ADA tables,
  figures and recommendations, kidney-function cut-offs, outcome benefits, special situations, Ramadan fasting and
  points where guidelines disagree)
- Tutor mode, timed exam simulation, a spaced-repetition review queue and a progress dashboard

Topics: insulin therapy, delivery and adjuncts in type 1 diabetes; stepwise drug choice in type 2 diabetes by
comorbidity (cardiovascular, heart failure, kidney disease, obesity); drug classes, their actions, adverse effects
and contraindications; starting, titrating and intensifying insulin in type 2 diabetes; deintensification, cost,
access and drug shortages; and special situations (pregnancy, cancer, glucocorticoids, pancreatic and
post-transplant diabetes, monogenic diabetes, Ramadan fasting).

## Where your progress is saved
Everything runs in your browser. Answers, scores and review dates are saved **only in the browser and device you
use** (browser local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing browser data, using a private window or switching
devices starts you fresh. Use **My study plan → Export progress** to download a CSV or JSON copy of your answers.

This copy is a plain static website: it has no AI tutor, no "Ask Claude" button and no account or cross-device sync.

## Sources and disclaimer
Answer keys follow the ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026; where a question relies on another guideline (for
example KDIGO, AACE, NICE or IDF-DAR for Ramadan fasting) the question names it. Doses follow the ADA tables or the
US product label.

An independent educational tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the American Diabetes Association, the
Endocrine Society or any exam body, and it is not medical advice. The questions are original practice items; check
clinical decisions, doses and local drug availability against current guidelines and product labels.
